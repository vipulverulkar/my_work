## 📱 Detailed Screen Capabilities & Features

### 1. 🏠 Home Dashboard
![Home Dashboard](./Screenshot/01_dashboard.png)
- Operational KPIs: Active Orders, Due Today, Total Revenue (₹), Pending Balance.
- Explicit Labeled Order Dates (`Booking Date` & `Delivery Date`).
- High-priority Low Stock Warning Alert banner.
- "Take New Order" FAB and simplified 5-tab bottom navigation.

### 2. 📜 Orders List & Channel Tracking
![Orders List Tracking](./Screenshot/02_orders.png)
- Color badges for order channels: **WhatsApp (🟢)**, **Phone Call (🔵)**, **Walk-in (🟣)**.
- Order sorting options: Delivery Date, Order ID, Total Amount, Balance Due.
- Order status filters: Received, Baking, Ready for Pickup, Delivered.
- Quick 1-tap WhatsApp receipt generator and native phone call dialer.

### 3. 📊 Financial Reports & Revenue Analytics
![Financial Reports](./Screenshot/03_reports.png)
- Time period filters: **All Time**, **Today**, **This Month**, **This Year**.
- Key Analytics: **Average Order Value (AOV)** and **Payment Collection Rate (%)**.
- **Sales by Bakery Category** breakdown with visual revenue share progress bars.
- Daily, Monthly, and Annual revenue breakdown trends.
- Outstanding payment directory with 1-tap WhatsApp balance reminders.

### 4. 🎂 Menu Catalog & Item Badges
![Menu Catalog](./Screenshot/04_menu.png)
- Product categories: Classic Cakes, Premium Collection, Brownies, Tea Cakes, Cookies.
- **Unlimited Custom Portion Sizes & Prices**: Dynamic custom sizes (e.g. 1.5 KG, 2 KG, 6-Inch, 8-Inch, Box of 6).
- Visual item category icons, multi-tier portion prices, and availability toggles.

### 5. 📝 Order Form & Delivery Notice Policy
![New Order Form](./Screenshot/06_new_order.png)
![Paginated Saved Client Picker](./Screenshot/07_saved_clients.png)
- Channel selection, searchable and paginated saved customer picker.
- **Delivery Policy**: Standard 1-day advance notice requirement with **⚡ Allow Same-Day Express Delivery** toggle switch.
- On-the-fly custom portion price overrides and "+ Custom Item" addition.

### 6. ⚙️ Settings, Themes & Backup Hub
![Settings & Backup Hub](./Screenshot/05_settings.png)
- Management hub shortcuts for Client CRM and Bakery Stock.
- 12 Bakery Color Themes with 3-color palette swatch previews.
- Multi-format backup & restore (**SQL .sql**, **CSV .csv**, **JSON .json**) with intelligent header content format detection.
